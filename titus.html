Welcome To Our Technical College

The College is located near Sagana-Nairobi Highway.

Our Technical College provides quality education and practical skills to students in different areas of technology, business, and engineering.

ICT Department

The ICT department teaches computer studies, programming, networking, web design and database management.

Business Department

The Business department provides skills in accounting, entrepreneurship, management and business studies.

Engineering Department

The Engineering department provides practical skills in electrical, mechanical and building engineering.
